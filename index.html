MEET FORMA

One interface.
A more human connection.

This concept brings a human avatar, a voice, and a small language model into one conversation.

SmolLM2 135M handles chat, Whisper Tiny handles listening, and Kitten Nano generates speech. A 14 KB trained lip-sync model animates a 3D face from the audio. This is face animation, not photorealistic video generation.

Chat and audio are processed on your device. Models download into browser storage on first use. GitHub Pages only serves the website files. Microphone access starts when you press the mic button.
