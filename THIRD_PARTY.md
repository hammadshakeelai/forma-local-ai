# Third-party components

- HeadAudio scripts, trained classifier, and Julia avatar: [met4citizen/HeadAudio](https://github.com/met4citizen/HeadAudio), MIT. Copied from the upstream `dist` and `avatars` folders. License preserved in `public/face/LICENSE`.
- TalkingHead: [met4citizen/TalkingHead](https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead), MIT, installed through npm.
- Three.js: [mrdoob/three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js), MIT, installed through npm.
- Transformers.js: [huggingface/transformers.js](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers.js), Apache-2.0, installed through npm.
- Kitten TTS WebGPU 0.1.1: [svenflow/kitten-tts-webgpu](https://github.com/svenflow/kitten-tts-webgpu), MIT as stated in the installed package LICENSE. Model weights come from KittenML and retain upstream licensing.
- SmolLM2 and Whisper ONNX weights are downloaded from their Hugging Face repositories at runtime; they are not included in this repository. Refer to their model cards for licensing and use information.

The application MIT license does not relicense these components.
